## weapons-atlas.png

Create ONE SQUARE transparent Unity sprite atlas, strict 3x3 grid of equally sized SQUARE cells, nine isolated cartoon weapon sprites. Match approved duck game's thick dark brown rounded outlines, warm shaded volumes, crisp simple readable silhouettes. EXACT order: row1 left a curved golden FEATHER BOOMERANG, center one cream EGG with tan speckles, right three cyan curved SOUND WAVE ARCS facing RIGHT like reference bottom right. Row2 left a perfectly CIRCULAR cyan magical AURA RING with a scalloped inner energy pattern and transparent center, center a red and cream toy ROCKET facing RIGHT with little fins and a small orange exhaust, right one perfectly CIRCULAR translucent SOAP BUBBLE with thin cyan/iridescent rim and white highlights, hollow transparent interior, clearly distinct from a solid blue XP orb. Row3 left a tight SPIRAL of THREE GOLDEN FEATHERS shaped like a tornado swirl, center an armored cream EGG in a small copper mechanical INCUBATOR cradle, right JAGGED DOUBLE CYAN SONIC WAVE arcs facing RIGHT, recognizably more intense than normal arcs. All subjects fully within their cell with at least 15% transparent margin, no overlapping cells, no exterior glow or shadows, no dark haze, no background outside subject outline: alpha zero there. No text, labels, numbers, grids, scenery, characters, diamonds or medical symbols. Aura and bubble must have equal horizontal and vertical diameter. This is one static atlas asset for real game integration.

Geração independente para corrigir o ruído da transparência. A bolha plana desta alternativa foi substituída após preferência do usuário; somente a aura é atribuída na entrega.

A minimal production-ready 2D game sprite atlas with exactly TWO circular ring sprites side by side, both perfectly round, same diameter, isolated on genuine transparent background. Canvas ratio 2:1 with two equal square cells and 15% transparent padding per cell. LEFT: magical energy aura ring, thick dark brown outer outline around a cyan annulus, twelve scalloped cyan shapes along its inside edge, perfectly empty transparent hole occupying inner 70% of diameter. RIGHT: soap bubble RIM ONLY, thin cyan annulus with dark brown outer hairline outline, two small SOLID cream reflection shapes printed ON the upper left RIM only, perfectly EMPTY TRANSPARENT HOLE occupying inner 85% of diameter. Use solid flat opaque colors on the rings, no gradients to transparency, no translucency, no texture or speckles. The interior holes and all exterior pixels are transparent with ZERO ALPHA. No noise, dithering, mist, particles, sparkles, shadows, stars, solid disk, white fill, text, labels or scene. Clean smooth cartoon graphic with brown outlines matching a warm cartoon duck game. Think two simple clean donut-shaped decals, not glass rendering.
